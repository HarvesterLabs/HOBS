RPB Research Status / Freeze Record
Version: v0.1
Status: Frozen / Research Record
Project: HOBS RPB Research
Date: 2026-09-08

============================================================
1. Purpose
============================================================

This document records the current status of the HOBS RPB Research and
defines the point at which the research is temporarily frozen.

The purpose of this freeze is not to declare every RPB mechanism solved.
It is to preserve the accumulated observations, distinguish confirmed
observations from unresolved questions, and prevent further excavation
from becoming a prerequisite for the already-completed HOBS MT5 VWAP.

============================================================
2. Current Decision
============================================================

RPB research is frozen at the current observation state.

No further excavation of historical RPB experiment numbers, source code,
logs, or undocumented conditions is required for the current HOBS MT5
VWAP project.

Future reopening is permitted if a later HOBS research requirement makes
RPB necessary again.

============================================================
3. Relationship to HOBS MT5 VWAP
============================================================

The HOBS MT5 VWAP has reached its completion point independently of a
complete explanation of MT5 historical-data expansion internals.

The current HOBS VWAP research established the required observation model
and implementation basis. Therefore, unresolved RPB questions are not
treated as blockers for the completed VWAP.

RPB is retained as supporting research, not as a remaining completion
condition for VWAP.

============================================================
4. What RPB Established
============================================================

RPB research investigated the distinction between:

- Requested access conditions
- Returned Range / Returned Detail
- Returned Series
- History State
- History Expansion
- Boundary-related access behavior
- Target-side dependency

A central research rule was maintained throughout the research:

    Request Success != Returned Range Change != History Expansion

History Expansion was not inferred merely from CopyRates success.
Observable History State changes such as SERIES_FIRSTDATE,
SERIES_BARS_COUNT, and SERIES_SYNCHRONIZED were treated separately.

The Boundary Chapter also established an observational framework around
SERIES_FIRSTDATE. This Boundary is treated as an experimental/observational
boundary, not as a proven absolute internal MT5 boundary.

============================================================
5. Boundary Chapter
============================================================

The Boundary Chapter was organized into four observation lines:

1. History Boundary Movement
2. Boundary Returned Detail
3. Boundary Returned Series
4. Boundary Transition Series

The chapter was summarized while preserving differences in experimental
conditions between the individual Evidence records.

The chapter does not claim to have identified the internal mechanism of
History Expansion.

============================================================
6. C Chapter
============================================================

The C Chapter investigated candidate conditions related to History
Expansion.

The research axes included:

- C-1 RequestStart
- C-2 RequestedBars / request range
- C-3 access to a possible unloaded candidate region
- C-4 Access Boundary
- C-5 successful access versus observable History State change

The conclusions are conditional on the tested experimental conditions.
They do not establish that History Expansion never occurs.

============================================================
7. C-2 Current Freeze Point
============================================================

A C-2-specific program was identified:

    HOBS_RPB_HistoryExpansionRange_Test_fixed_v2

The current stored program fixes RequestStart relative to the initial
SERIES_FIRSTDATE and varies RequestedBars.

Current stored RequestedBars:

    1 / 10 / 100 / 1000 / 10000

Current stored execution condition:

    5 ranges x 120 attempts = 600 planned observations

On 2026-09-08, the current program was executed on USDJPY_z, M1.

The execution produced:

    600 observations
    0 successes
    600 failures

The observed requests returned Error 4401 under the tested conditions.

Because Access Success was not established, this execution does NOT prove
that RequestedBars caused or failed to cause History Expansion. It records
that the current C-2 apparatus did not establish successful access under
those conditions.

============================================================
8. Unresolved Historical Numbers
============================================================

Historical RPB materials contain references to observation totals such as
840 and 720.

The currently stored C-2 fixed_v2 program does not explain the historical
840 directly because its current RequestedBars list contains five values,
producing 600 planned observations at 120 attempts per value.

Therefore:

- 840 is not reclassified as false.
- 720 is not reclassified as false.
- Their original experimental provenance is not currently established.
- These numbers remain unresolved historical records.

The research will not modify the current program merely to reproduce a
historical total.

If RPB is reopened in the future, the provenance of these historical
numbers can be investigated separately.

============================================================
9. What Is NOT Claimed
============================================================

This freeze record does NOT claim:

- that MT5 History Expansion has been completely explained;
- that Error 4401 has a single proven causal explanation in every context;
- that History Expansion never occurs;
- that the historical 840 or 720 records are false;
- that all RPB experiments were performed under identical conditions;
- that Returned Range, Returned Series, History State, and History
  Expansion are the same phenomenon.

============================================================
10. Evidence Policy After Freeze
============================================================

Existing RPB documents, experiment programs, logs, screenshots, and
Evidence records are preserved.

They should not be silently rewritten merely to make the historical
record internally prettier.

Where an inconsistency or unresolved value exists, it should be recorded
as unresolved or condition-dependent.

The purpose of preservation is reproducibility of the research history,
not retrospective normalization.

============================================================
11. Reopening Conditions
============================================================

RPB may be reopened if one of the following becomes necessary:

- HOBS requires a deeper understanding of MT5 historical-data behavior;
- a future HOBS component depends directly on History Expansion;
- a new reproducible Evidence record contradicts the current boundary;
- historical experiment provenance becomes necessary for a later research
  or implementation decision.

Until such a condition arises, RPB remains frozen.

============================================================
12. Current HOBS Research Direction
============================================================

With RPB frozen, research priority returns to the main HOBS observation
architecture and the next required observation axes.

RPB is therefore treated as preserved research infrastructure and
historical Evidence, rather than as an active blocker.

============================================================
13. Final Freeze Statement
============================================================

RPB is not considered "solved."

RPB is considered:

    OBSERVED
    DOCUMENTED
    PRESERVED
    UNRESOLVED IN PART
    FROZEN FOR NOW

The unresolved portions remain available for future investigation, but
they do not prevent the completed HOBS MT5 VWAP from being treated as
complete.
